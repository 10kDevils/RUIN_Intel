# RUIN Intel

See something, say something: one button reports what you see and where. Every RUIN member gets an alert, a feed entry and a pin on the map that fades as the report ages. Plus world boss and world buff timers.

Part of the RUIN addon suite. Requires **RUIN Core** (0.3.0 or later). With **RUIN Warfront** installed, enemy sightings contest nearby objectives and "under attack" calls show on the Warfront HUD.

## The experience
- **Where it lives:** the Intel page in the RUIN window (Report, Timers), alerts, map and minimap pins, `/intel`, and key bindings for reporting an enemy group and for "I'm under attack".
- **Reporting:** press Enemy group, Under attack!, World boss, Rare, Buff drop or Other, with an optional detail like "8 Horde, heading north". Your location is added.
- **Under attack:** calls for help with your position, once a minute at most. Bind it to a Steam Deck back button.
- **Map pins:** reports with a position are pinned on the world map and minimap and fade over 30 minutes. Toggle with *Map pins*.
- **Timers:** press *Now* when a world boss dies or a world buff drops, or announce a buff drop ahead of time ("in 5 min"). Everyone gets the alert and a one-minute warning. Classic respawn windows are shown as a guide.
- **Commands:** `/intel enemy|attack|boss|rare|buff|other [detail]`, `/intel timer <name> [minutes]`.

## What it shares with RUIN
Reports you send (kind, detail, place and coordinates), and timer updates.

## Notes
Reports are manual; Intel doesn't watch the combat log, which WoW Forever restricts.
